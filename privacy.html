Privacy notice

Draft for legal review. Not in effect.

What we collect

When you send a request, we keep your name, email address and anything else you choose to enter: phone number, field and message.

For staff, we keep login records and a history of editorial actions.

How we use it

We use request details to answer you. We do not sell them.

Published profiles

A profile is published only with the person's permission on record. A person can withdraw permission, and the profile then leaves the registry. Copies held by search engines or other sites may take longer to change.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your details by sending a request.
