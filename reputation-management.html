Reputation management

A separate service from our publishing. When prospective clients or patients search your name, what they find can shape their first impression.

The problem

  • People search your name before they call you.
  • They may find little, or results that are out of date.
  • A few unfavorable results can sit above years of good work.

The solution

We work to improve how accurate, positive information about your background, experience and achievements is presented and found. We do not promise to delete anything.

What the service includes

  • Reputation auditWhat a client or patient finds when they search your name, on Google and in AI assistants.
  • Positive professional contentAccurate material about your background, experience and achievements, approved by you.
  • Digital visibility and SEOSearch optimization and better professional profiles, so accurate information is easier to find.
  • Monitoring and managementSearch visibility monitoring and ongoing management, with progress reports.
