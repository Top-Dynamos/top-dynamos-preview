Digital and hardcopy publications

Your approved biography, published in the formats your package includes: digital, printed, in the registry and on your own website.

The formats, side by side

Each format is built from the same approved text. Your written order confirms which ones you receive. Not every package includes every format.

  • Digital biography

    What it is
    Your approved biography as a digital document
    How you receive it
    Shared by link or attachment, read on any device
    Your review
    You read the full draft and approve it

  • Hardcover biography

    What it is
    Your approved biography in a bound book
    How you receive it
    Printed and sent to you; specifications confirmed with your order
    Your review
    You approve the text and a proof before printing

  • Printed publications

    What it is
    Your profile in a printed edition or booklet, where your package includes it
    How you receive it
    Printed copy sent to you
    Your review
    You approve your page before printing

  • Registry profile

    What it is
    A structured page in the searchable registry
    How you receive it
    Online, at an address in the registry
    Your review
    You approve the text and the facts shown

  • Personal website

    What it is
    A multi-page site under your name
    How you receive it
    Online, at an address confirmed in your order
    Your review
    You approve every page before launch

How you review your material

You receive the full text first. Mark every change on the draft, or tell us on a call. We correct it and send it back until you approve it in writing. For anything printed, you also approve a proof that shows exactly how the page will look.

What we do not promise

Our publications are published by Top Dynamos. They are not inclusion in an outside publication, and they are not an independent honor. Printed specifications and delivery dates are confirmed with your order.

How publishing works

  1. 1Information

    You send your CV, licenses or bar details, education, positions, publications and a photo. A short call fills the gaps.

  2. 2Research and drafting

    We check your details against your documents and public sources, then write the biography, release and site pages.

  3. 3Your review

    You read every word. Mark anything that is wrong, missing or not how you would say it.

  4. 4Approval

    You approve the final text in writing. Nothing goes out before that.

  5. 5Publication

    The approved work goes live: website, registry profile and press release, as your package includes.

  6. 6Delivery

    Printed pieces in your package, such as a hardcover, or a certificate and award for clients who meet eligibility, are produced from the approved text and sent to you.

  7. 7Revisions

    If a fact changes later, tell us. How many updates are included is set out in your agreement.
