Editorial standards

What a profile must meet before it goes live, and what happens after.

What our process requires

A profile needs a source, a biography and a permission record confirmed by a second member of staff before it can be published. Changes are logged with who made them. This is an internal check, not legal proof of consent.

What a profile is not

It is not a license check or an independent investigation. Sources show where information came from. They do not mean every statement has been independently verified.

Corrections and removal

If something is wrong, tell us and we will review it. If a person withdraws permission, their profile leaves the registry. Copies held by search engines or other sites may take longer to change.

Payment and publication

Services are separate from what a profile says. Paying for a service does not let anyone publish a statement that cannot be sourced.
