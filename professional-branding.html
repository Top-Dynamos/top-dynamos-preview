Professional biographies

One complete, accurate account of your career, written from your documents and approved by you. It is the source for everything else we publish.

What a professional biography is

A biography is not a resume in sentences. It explains where you trained, what you focus on, what you have built and why it matters, in language a patient, client or colleague understands. Every fact traces back to a document you gave us.

It comes in more than one length: a full version for your website and profile, a short version for introductions and event programs, and a one-line description for listings.

What you provide

Most of the work is ours. You send what you have, and we ask for anything missing.

  • Your CV or resume

    The backbone of the biography: education, training, positions and dates.

  • Credentials

    Licenses, board certifications or bar admissions, so each one can be stated exactly.

  • Published work

    Articles, books, talks, research and media mentions, with links where they exist.

  • Your own account

    A short call about what you focus on, what you are proud of and how you like to be described.

  • A professional photo

    One you already use, or one you approve.

How your biography is written and approved

  1. 1Information

    You send your CV, licenses or bar details, education, positions, publications and a photo. A short call fills the gaps.

  2. 2Research and drafting

    We check your details against your documents and public sources, then write the biography, release and site pages.

  3. 3Your review

    You read every word. Mark anything that is wrong, missing or not how you would say it.

  4. 4Approval

    You approve the final text in writing. Nothing goes out before that.

  5. 5Publication

    The approved work goes live: website, registry profile and press release, as your package includes.

  6. 6Delivery

    Printed pieces in your package, such as a hardcover, or a certificate and award for clients who meet eligibility, are produced from the approved text and sent to you.

  7. 7Revisions

    If a fact changes later, tell us. How many updates are included is set out in your agreement.
