Publishing

Biographies and publishing materials prepared for your approval, online and in print.

The problem

Professional experience is often spread across websites, documents and public profiles.

The solution

One documented account of your career. Depending on your package, that can include a biography, digital and hardcopy publications, a website and a press release.

What you receive

  • Biography and bookA professionally written biography, published online and in a premium hardcover book.
  • Digital and hardcopy publicationsYour work published online and in print, in the formats your package includes.
  • Press releasesNews about your work, written with you, published online and linked to your profile.
  • Personal websiteA site of your own that shows your career, achievements, credentials and background.
