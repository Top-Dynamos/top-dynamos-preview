The problem: your career is online in pieces

Physicians and attorneys rarely have no online presence. They have too many fragments of one. Each was written by someone else, at a different time, for a different purpose, and none of them tells the whole story.

  • Scattered

    Your career lives on a hospital or firm page, a practice site, a bar or board listing, LinkedIn, old news stories and a dozen directories. Each holds a fragment.

  • Inconsistent

    The fragments disagree. One says you are still at a practice you left. Another lists the wrong specialty. A third gives an old address.

  • Incomplete

    Fellowships, clerkships, publications, teaching, military service and leadership roles sit in your CV, where nobody outside your office sees them.

  • Written by others

    Most of what is online about you was written by a directory, an employer or a database. Little of it is in your words or checked by you.

A cardiologist is board certified in two areas and holds privileges at two hospitals. One directory shows a single certification and the old hospital. The hospital page is a three-line summary. A patient sees neither the training nor the focus.

A personal injury attorney has practiced for thirty years, owns the firm and teaches continuing legal education. The firm page lists a law school and a bar admission. Directory ads from other firms fill the rest of the results.

A family physician has cared for the same community for twenty years and speaks three languages. Listing sites show a phone number and a star rating, and nothing about who she is.

It looks different in every field

  • General practitioners, cardiologists, podiatrists, surgeons and academic physicians
  • Personal injury, criminal defense, estate planning, family and business attorneys

Why it matters

Patients, clients, referral sources and colleagues research a professional before they make contact. What they find in the first minute shapes whether they call, and fragments that disagree raise questions you never get the chance to answer.

  1. 1

    Referral

    A patient or client hears your name from a friend, a doctor or another lawyer.

  2. 2

    Search

    They search your name, often on a phone, and often before they call.

  3. 3

    First screen

    They read whatever appears first: directories, reviews, old pages and ads.

  4. 4

    Decision

    They call, or they choose someone whose background was easier to understand.

How publishing works

  1. 1Information

    You send your CV, licenses or bar details, education, positions, publications and a photo. A short call fills the gaps.

  2. 2Research and drafting

    We check your details against your documents and public sources, then write the biography, release and site pages.

  3. 3Your review

    You read every word. Mark anything that is wrong, missing or not how you would say it.

  4. 4Approval

    You approve the final text in writing. Nothing goes out before that.

  5. 5Publication

    The approved work goes live: website, registry profile and press release, as your package includes.

  6. 6Delivery

    Printed pieces in your package, such as a hardcover, or a certificate and award for clients who meet eligibility, are produced from the approved text and sent to you.

  7. 7Revisions

    If a fact changes later, tell us. How many updates are included is set out in your agreement.

Questions

Is this an advertisement or an honor?

It is a paid publishing service. Your biography, website and release are prepared and published because you engaged us, after editorial checks. Nothing here is presented as an independent honor or ranking.

Which items are in my package?

Your written order lists exactly what you receive. Not every item is in every package, and printed pieces are produced only after you approve the text.

How much do I have to write?

Very little. Send your CV and documents, and answer our questions on a short call. We do the writing, and you correct it.

Will this move me up in Google?

More accurate pages under your name usually help people find the right information about you. Search engines decide what to show, so no position is promised.

Can I change something after it is published?

Yes. Tell us what changed. How many updates are included is set out in your agreement.

Is reputation management included?

No. Reputation management is a separate service with its own page. You can buy either one without the other.
