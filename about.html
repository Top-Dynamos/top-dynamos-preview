About Top Dynamos

We publish profiles of working professionals and help them present their careers accurately.

What we do

The registry lets anyone look up a physician, attorney or executive who has a published profile. We also publish for professionals: biographies, digital and hardcopy publications, press releases and websites. Reputation management is a separate service.

Rules every profile must meet

  • It lists at least one source on the page.
  • The person's permission is on record, and a second member of staff has confirmed that record.
  • If permission is withdrawn, the profile leaves the registry.
