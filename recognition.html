Professional recognition

A personalized award and certificate, for clients who meet eligibility, as part of a paid package.

What this is, plainly

The award and certificate are part of a paid service. They are not an independent honor, and paying for a package does not make someone eligible. Eligibility is confirmed before enrollment.

The image is a concept illustration, not a photograph of a finished award.

  • Personalized award

    Made for you, with your name and your field. One award per person.

  • Certificate of recognition

    A certificate that records the recognition of your professional achievements.

  • Details checked first

    We check your name, credentials and field line against your documents before anything is made.
