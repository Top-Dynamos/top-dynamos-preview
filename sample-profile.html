Sample profile

The layout every registry profile follows. This is a template with placeholders, not a real person.

Sample layout. Every bracket is replaced with the person's own details, checked against their documents, before a real profile is published.

[Full name], MD

[Specialty], [City, State]

Record [number]. On record since [date].

Biography

[Two or three paragraphs written from your documents and your own account: where you trained, what you focus on, what you have built, and what matters to you in your work. You approve every sentence.]

Current position
[Title], [Practice or hospital], [City, State]
Specialty
[Specialty and any subspecialty, as you state it]
Board certification
[Board name and certification, from your certificate]
Medical school
[School], [Degree], [Year]
Residency
[Program], [Years]
Fellowship
[Program], [Years]
Hospital affiliations
[Hospital names, current and past]
Publications
[Articles, books or research you choose, with links]
Teaching and service
[Faculty roles, committees, military or community service]
Recognition
[Honors from other bodies, with their source. Paid recognition is labeled as paid.]
Languages
[Languages you speak with patients]

Sources

  • [Your CV, provided by you]
  • [State medical board lookup]
  • [Board certification lookup]
  • [Hospital or practice page]

A profile is not a license check. To confirm a license or disciplinary history, contact the state medical board.
