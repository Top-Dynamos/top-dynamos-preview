Why a site under your own name

  • Your name, not only your firm or practice

    Your business site sells the practice. A site under your own name tells your story as a professional, and stays with you if you move.

  • One link that tells the whole story

    Send it to a new patient, a client, a referral source or an event organizer. Link it from your practice or firm site and your LinkedIn.

  • Built from your approved biography

    The same checked facts as your registry profile and press releases, so nothing disagrees.

  • Made for phones first

    Most people will open it on a phone, so it is designed and tested there first.

What the site usually includes

The exact pages follow your career. These are the sections most sites have.

HomeAbout and careerEducation and trainingRecognitionPublications and mediaGallery and contact

  • Home

    Your name, role and city, a short introduction and a clear way to reach your practice or firm.

  • About and career

    Your full biography, positions held and the path that led to your work today.

  • Education and training

    Degrees, residency, fellowship, clerkships or certifications, as you document them.

  • Recognition

    Honors from other bodies with their source. Paid recognition is labeled as paid, for clients who meet eligibility.

  • Publications and media

    Articles, books, talks and press releases, linked to the originals.

  • Gallery and contact

    Photos you approve, and the contact details you choose to show.

How your website is built

  1. 1Information

    You send your CV, licenses or bar details, education, positions, publications and a photo. A short call fills the gaps.

  2. 2Research and drafting

    We check your details against your documents and public sources, then write the biography, release and site pages.

  3. 3Your review

    You read every word. Mark anything that is wrong, missing or not how you would say it.

  4. 4Approval

    You approve the final text in writing. Nothing goes out before that.

  5. 5Publication

    The approved work goes live: website, registry profile and press release, as your package includes.

  6. 6Delivery

    Printed pieces in your package, such as a hardcover, or a certificate and award for clients who meet eligibility, are produced from the approved text and sent to you.

  7. 7Revisions

    If a fact changes later, tell us. How many updates are included is set out in your agreement.

Questions

What address will the site have?

An address in your name where one is available. Your written order confirms the address and who holds it.

Can you match my firm or practice brand?

We design a look that fits you and does not clash with your practice or firm. If your employer has brand rules, send them.

Can I make changes later?

Yes. Tell us what changed. How many updates are included is set out in your agreement.

Does the site include patient or client reviews?

No reviews or testimonials are written or arranged by us. You can link to review pages you choose.

Will the site rank on Google?

A clear site under your name gives search engines an accurate page about you. Search engines decide rankings, so no position is promised.

Tell us about your career and we will plan the pages with you.
